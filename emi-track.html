Privacy Policy

App: Expense Tracker — Loan & Tax Calculator ("the App")
Last updated: September 3, 2026

This App is designed to work fully offline and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or device data with the developer or any third party.

1. Data Collection

The App does not request internet access and does not contain any analytics, advertising, or tracking libraries. We do not collect, view, or have access to any information you enter into the App.

2. Data Storage

All information you enter (such as expenses, loan details, and tax calculations) is stored locally on your device only, using Android's standard app storage (shared preferences). This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export or share it (see below).

3. Data Export / Backup

The App includes an optional backup/export feature that lets you save your data to a file of your choosing and share it using apps already installed on your device (for example, email or cloud storage apps). This action is always initiated by you, and the developer does not receive a copy of this data.

4. Permissions

The App only requests the storage/file access permissions required to save or open a backup file that you choose. It does not request permissions for camera, contacts, location, SMS, microphone, or any other sensitive device data, and it does not use the internet permission.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-processing services.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data at all.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository: github.com/KULDEEPAUGUS/EMI_LOAN_APP.
