# Privacy Policy for DocuSize & Scan

_Last updated: June 12, 2026_

**DocuSize & Scan** ("the App", "we", "us") is a utility application that
lets users scan documents, resize images, and work with PDF files. This
policy explains what data the App accesses and how it is handled.

## 1. Data we collect

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

The App performs every operation locally on your device. It does not
contain user accounts, analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or any network
calls that send your files or usage data to a server.

## 2. Files you process

Documents, images, and PDFs that you open inside the App remain on your
device at all times. We do not upload them anywhere. When you export or
share a file, you choose the destination yourself through the standard
Android share sheet.

## 3. Permissions the App requests

| Permission | Why it is requested |
|---|---|
| Camera | Used only while you are using the document scanner. |
| Photos / Media / Files | Used to read source files and save the files you create. |

These permissions are requested at the moment you first use the relevant
feature, and you may revoke them at any time in Android settings.

## 4. Third-party services

The App does not include third-party analytics, advertising, crash
reporting, or tracking SDKs.

The document scanner feature uses **Google ML Kit on-device models**.
These models run locally and do not transmit your scanned images.
See: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms

## 5. Children

The App is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children
under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from any user.

## 6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same
URL with a new "Last updated" date.
